Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 October 2026. This notice should be reviewed again when the final payment and verification providers are connected.

Information we process

Account details, name, date of birth, country, address, conversation preferences, subscription status, messages and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Payments

Stripe handles payment credentials. MinaZhang does not store or display full card numbers, CVVs, online-banking passwords, PINs or full bank-account credentials. We may receive limited billing information Stripe makes available, such as billing name, bank/card brand, country, payment status, transaction/subscription identifiers and masked or partial account details.

Who can see billing information

Limited billing information received from the payment provider is intended for the MinaZhang operator for account administration, support, fraud prevention and billing questions. It is not shown to other members through the service. Payment providers and other processors may also process information under their own privacy notices and legal obligations.

Messages

Private messages are available to the member who sent them and to the MinaZhang operator for the purpose of providing the one-to-one chat service, moderation, safety and support.

Profile verification

Name, date of birth, country and address are collected before access to the member area for the stated age, access and security purposes. For stronger identity verification, a dedicated provider may be used; where possible, MinaZhang should retain the verification result/reference rather than unnecessary copies of passports or other identity documents. We aim to retain the verification result and reference rather than unnecessary copies of identity documents.

Your choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or other rights regarding your personal information.
