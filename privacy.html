Demo Privacy Notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What this demo processes

If you create an account, the authentication service processes the email address and password you provide. A profile may store your display name and selected preferences.

Do not submit sensitive information

This demonstration does not need or request payment credentials, card numbers, CVV/CVC, IBANs, banking passwords, PINs, passport images or identity documents. Never enter those details.

Payments

No payment provider is connected for customer checkout. The €0.99/year amount is illustrative only; no charge or subscription is created.

Messages

If the private-room demo is enabled, messages may be stored to demonstrate the chat feature and may be visible to the site operator.

Data requests

Use the website contact method to ask about access, correction or deletion of demo account information. Use test-only details when registering.
