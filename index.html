Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

Your first month is free. After the trial, the subscription is €4.99/month and renews automatically until cancelled.

Who can join?

The service is for adults aged 18 or over. An age/identity check may be required before chat access.

How does payment work?

Payment details should be entered through the selected payment provider's checkout. MinaZhang should only receive the account and subscription information needed to operate the service.

Is the chat private?

Each subscriber gets a separate conversation. Production access must be protected with authentication, authorization and database security rules.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel before the next billing date using the cancellation flow supplied by the payment provider or account area.
